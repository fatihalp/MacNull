Empty, at rest

rejected
checks 16/16insufficient evidence · low

A hand-thrown lipped bowl, emptied and set down: null as a vessel you pour out with care, not one you smash.

  • An object with character, not a UI icon: the spout makes a common bowl specific, and the empty interior is a real hole, so whatever is behind it shows through
  • Warm and calm in a category of brooms, sparkles and purple gradients
  • The word is visibly drawn: every stem in macnull, including the a, lands on the same soft pebble foot, and the second l rests a step lower, so the ll reads as a deliberate pair
  • Celadon teal keeps clear of the amber the app uses for Check First warnings
  • A bowl is still a kitchen object: some will read food, a gravy boat or a soup app before they read null
  • The spout could be read as a speech-bubble tail when the bowl is small
  • Fraunces 600 is wide, so the word needs room in a narrow toolbar
  • The lower second l is subtle and may look like an accident until it is explained
Details & mockups

Null as a vessel emptied with care: a lipped bowl pours out deliberately, never smashes. Celadon-teal glaze on warm ink and paper gives calm craft rather than clinical tooling; teal also keeps clear of the amber the app uses for Check First warnings.

The emptied stem

shortlisted
checks 15/15insufficient evidence · low

MacNull set in a heavy engineered grotesque where every stem top is cut at one rising angle and the final l is left hollow: present but emptied, the way a cleaned file still sits in the Trash, and the icon pair reads both as ll and as a 1 next to a 0.

  • the move comes from this name's own letters (the double ll), so it is ownable and needs no explanation
  • one chisel angle on every free stem top (M, N, u, l) makes the whole word read as drawn, not typeset
  • the icon has a developer double reading (1 and 0, set and null) without being a letter in a tile
  • the hollow l thins to a grey stroke below about 16 px cap height, where the word reads as plain MacNull
  • the icon pair could still be taken for a 10 or two buildings before it is seen as ll
  • on light paper the mint signal colour lacks contrast, so the master is graphite-only and mint lives on dark grounds
Details & mockups

For a coined name the word is the identity. The ll pair is this name's own opportunity: two parallel stems, one solid, one emptied, says null and also still there, restorable from the Trash.

The drawn null

shortlisted
checks 16/17insufficient evidence · low

A zero struck with a backslash: \0, the null character developers know, which is also the N of MacNull drawn as one heavy solid form; every free terminal of the word is cut on the same backslash angle.

  • The symbol says null in a developer's own notation and doubles as an N, so it is ownable without being a lone initial
  • One angle runs through symbol and word, so the system reads as built, not assembled
  • Heavy filled silhouette holds up as an app icon on an ink tile and at 16 px
  • The narrow blade counters close up around 16 px and the mark reads as a plain dark pill
  • Some will first read the symbol as an N in a rounded box rather than a zero
  • The faceted terminals vanish below about 24 px, so the small word is close to stock JetBrains Mono
Details & mockups

Developers know the slashed zero as the character that cannot be mistaken; MacNull's promise is the same exactness about what gets emptied
